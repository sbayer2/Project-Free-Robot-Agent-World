## Findings

1. **Test coverage (worth fixing before F36 cites the script):**

   - So on a plain `pip install -e ".[dev]"` **all 8 tests skip silently**, including the ones that don't touch Fisher (G1 list, WITHHELD wording, column counts).
   - Simplest fix: compute Fisher from the hypergeometric with `math.comb`, as your own check and the test already do, drop the scipy import, and drop the module-level importorskip.
