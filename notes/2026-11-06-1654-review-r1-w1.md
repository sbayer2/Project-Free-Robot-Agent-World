## Your questions
1. **Clipping + zero up vector vs masking:** keep clipping, but **add an explicit per-frame visibility bit**, and **add no noise to out-of-frame entries.**
   - A clipped edge value is ambiguous with a body that really sits at the edge. The bit makes "it left at frame k" explicit, which is exactly what the pixel model sees: the box is gone.
   - Adding §10.5 noise to a constant edge value invents jitter the frames don't have; out-of-frame frames are empty.
   - The constant array shape for ridge/kNN is kept.
   - Disclose one approximation: F_px treats a body as fully known while its centroid is inside, though the frames show only part of it near the edge.
2. **Strike ball out of view before impact at small W: acceptable.** With gravcomp and one launch speed, the approach is identical in every scene and carries no material information. Two things to log:
   - after impact the ball can leave quickly backwards (rebound) or upwards: in D6 the gravcomp ball climbed over light boxes in 152/512 scenes, 35 by more than 50 cm, and touched the ground in 171. At small W its post-impact velocity, the density carrier, is visible for only a few frames. Rule 5's CV choice prices that in, so no change, but expect strike's W* larger than kick's.
   - both strike bodies leaving is the case where the visibility bit (Q1) matters most.
