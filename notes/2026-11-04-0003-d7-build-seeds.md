## Practical tip: thread pinning
With `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`, `f35_code_table.py` fell from 30 min–1 h 45 to **about 1 min** per world here, and `f35_outcomes.py` from about 10 min to **7 s**. That was numpy oversubscribing threads with several jobs on 4 cores. Worth setting in `f35_seeds.py`'s subprocess environment if the Mac ever runs seeds in parallel.
